Cycle settings

Changes apply on the next cycle. Default retreat is toward the base, 40° upward, within the selected cone. The forward approach comes from the base side and down to the grasp. The captured position sets the horizontal bearing independently of wrist angle. Release uses an independent bounded linear opening ramp, with no minimum dwell; retreat starts on the next tick after the full-open command. The gripper stays open until the last 0.02 m, then closes at a constant rate through arrival to full calibrated close. Speed settings are ceilings; IK bounds can keep actual speeds lower.

Only autonomous forward approach + close episodes are recorded, with left wrist video. Grasp outcome is unverified; there is no object success sensing. Human adjustment is never recorded.
