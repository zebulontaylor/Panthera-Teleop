# Archived material

Earlier bounded approach/motion experiments and their JSON results and camera captures are preserved in `experiments/`. Board and headset images are in `reference/`; downloaded setup material and superseded configs are in `setup/`.

These are historical artifacts. Experimental scripts may reference their former locations and are not supported launchers. Use the workspace `start.sh` and active `app/`, `config/`, and `scripts/` instead. No recordings or calibration evidence were deleted during cleanup.
